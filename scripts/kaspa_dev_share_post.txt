TN10 integrator rehearsal — public repo for Core, SDK, and exchange teams

https://github.com/explife365/kaspa-frontier-engine

Toccata is live on testnet-10. This crate rehearses what integrators need next: owned-node health, DAA-confirmed deposits and withdrawals, durable wRPC ingestion, and covenant proof bundles.

What is working today on TN10:
- N-of-M owned-node health gate (node 1 + node 2, fail-closed until 2/2 green)
- SQLite-backed deposits, withdrawals, and webhook outbox with idempotency keys
- wRPC live ingestion with subscribe-ack journal replay (rusty-kaspa#939 pattern)
- tn10-proof for counter, timelock vault, and restricted swap lineage checks
- Bounded tn10-covenant-rpc shim while getUtxosByCovenantId is not on kaspad

One command rehearsal:
  powershell -File scripts/tn10_rehearsal.ps1 -Covenant

Next unlock for public covenant txids: kaspa-python-sdk#78 (computeBudget in TransactionInput.to_dict). Three SilverScript reference apps are ready; repro gate:
  python scripts/tn10_sdk_gate.py --json

Helpful reviews: PR #78 on kaspa-python-sdk, rusty-kaspa#1073 and #1102 for integrator context.

Not in scope: consensus patches, DAGKnight activation, or L1 EVM/stables.
